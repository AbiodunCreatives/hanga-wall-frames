Our story

We Believe Every Wall
Has a Story to Tell.

Hanga was born from a simple obsession: walls should not be blank. They should speak — of memories, of beauty, of who you are.

Why We Started Hanga

It started with a bare wall in an apartment. The kind of wall that stared back at you every morning with nothing to say. We asked ourselves — why is it so hard to get a beautiful, custom-framed print in Nigeria?

Between searching for good quality, waiting weeks for imports, and paying absurd prices, most people just gave up and lived with blank walls. We thought that was unacceptable.

So we built Hanga. A place where you pick the image, choose the frame, and we handle the rest — printed, framed, and delivered to your door.
