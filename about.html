Why We Started

It started with a bare wall, and a simple question: why is a custom-framed print so hard to get in Nigeria?

Searching for quality, waiting weeks for imports, paying too much — most people gave up. So we built Hanga. You pick the image and the frame; we print, frame and deliver it to your door.
