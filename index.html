Delivery & FAQs

What does Hanga sell?

Custom wall frames, printed and delivered across Nigeria. Pick a gallery print (50+ available) or upload your own photo.

How much do frames cost, and what sizes do you offer?

Prices are per frame and include the style — black lacquer, clean white, or borderless:

  • 12 × 16" (30 × 40 cm) — ₦7,000
  • 16 × 20" (40 × 50 cm) — ₦9,000
  • 16 × 24" (40 × 60 cm) — ₦11,000
  • 20 × 24" (50 × 60 cm) — ₦13,000

Delivery is added at checkout.

What is a Pair, and how does the ₦500 saving work?

A Pair is two frames that hang together, in the same size and style. Pairs are ₦500 off:

  • 12 × 16" Pair — ₦13,500
  • 16 × 20" Pair — ₦17,500
  • 16 × 24" Pair — ₦21,500
  • 20 × 24" Pair — ₦25,500
Do you deliver to Lagos, Abuja and the rest of Nigeria? How long does it take?

Yes — nationwide within 3 business days. A flat fee by region, paid at checkout — one fee per order:

  • ₦2,500 South-West — Lagos, Ogun, Oyo (including Ibadan), Osun, Ondo, Ekiti
  • ₦4,500 East & South — Abia, Anambra, Ebonyi, Enugu, Imo, Akwa Ibom, Bayelsa, Cross River, Delta, Edo, Rivers (including Port Harcourt)
  • ₦6,000 North & FCT — FCT (Abuja), Benue, Kogi, Kwara, Nasarawa, Niger, Plateau, Adamawa, Bauchi, Borno, Gombe, Taraba, Yobe, Jigawa, Kaduna, Kano, Katsina, Kebbi, Sokoto, Zamfara
How do I pay?

Pay by card, bank transfer or USSD via Paystack at checkout. Tap Pay and Paystack opens on the same page. You get a receipt and an order reference. Email is optional, and used for the Paystack receipt.

Can I frame my own photo? What file should I send?

Yes. Upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP (up to 10 MB) or a PDF (up to 20 MB). For the sharpest print, use the original photo, not a screenshot or a WhatsApp copy.

Do the black, white and borderless options cost different amounts?

No. All three styles are the same price — you only pay for the size.

How do I order?

Open the customizer, choose a Single or a Pair, pick or upload an image, choose a size and style, and pay via Paystack. We print, frame and deliver within 3 business days.

What is your refund and returns policy?

Damaged or wrong item on arrival — send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we will reprint and redeliver at no cost to you.

Print quality concern — if the colour, sharpness or finish is significantly different from what was previewed, contact us within 3 days. We will assess it and reprint.

Change of mind — because every frame is printed and built to order, we cannot accept returns for change of mind once production has started. Please review your size, image and style carefully before confirming your order.

Upload issues — if you uploaded a high quality image and the print came out blurry or cropped unexpectedly, we will work with you to get it right. We always flag low-resolution uploads before printing when we can.

To start a claim, message us on WhatsApp or email hangaframes@gmail.com with your order reference and a photo of the issue. We aim to respond within one business day.

How do I contact Hanga?

Email hangaframes@gmail.com, WhatsApp +234 815 153 3349, or Instagram @hangaframes_.

Is there a walk-in shop?

No walk-in shop. Order online at hangaframes.art — we deliver nationwide. For questions, use the contacts above.
