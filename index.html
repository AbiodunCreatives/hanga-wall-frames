Delivery & FAQs

Where do you deliver, and how long does it take?

Hanga is based in Ado Ekiti, Ekiti State. Delivery within Ekiti and Ondo State takes 1 to 2 business days. Delivery to other states takes 2 to 3 business days.

How much is delivery?

We will confirm your delivery fee after your order.

How do I pay?

Place your order at checkout and you'll get an order reference. We then confirm your total (frame + delivery) and payment details with you before we start framing.

Do the black, white and borderless options cost different amounts?

No. All three styles are the same price — you only pay for the size.

Can I frame my own photo?

Yes. Upload a JPG, PNG or WEBP (up to 10 MB) in the customiser. For the sharpest print, use the original photo rather than a screenshot or a copy sent over WhatsApp.
